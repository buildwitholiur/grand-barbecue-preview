ABOUT

Each year, we bring together master grillers and barbecue artisans to showcase their signature styles and creative fire. From smoky classics to bold new twists, this is where taste buds come to dance. It’s a one-day journey of bold bites and good vibes.

Every summer, the magic happens in the heart of our community. Smoke rises, drums beat, laughter rings out, and the aroma of seasoned meats fills the air. Welcome to The Grand Barbecue, a celebration of culture, flavor, and togetherness.

Expect music, laughter, vendors, and unforgettable moments shared around open flames. Whether you're discovering a new flavor, vibing to great tunes, or just spending quality time with friends and family, the Grand Barbecue is where it all comes together.

A CAUSE

Behind the smoke and music, there’s always a cause.
Each edition of the Grand Barbecue supports a humanitarian mission.

Whether it’s delivering aid to communities in crisis or uplifting a local initiative, your plate serves a greater purpose. When you show up, eat well, and enjoy the day, you’re also helping someone rebuild theirs.
